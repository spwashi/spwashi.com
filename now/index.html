What changed · November 17–19

  • Calmer first paint: Stopped late scripts from resizing cards, nav links, chips, and image hints after load across all 212 routes (passive arrival-geometry couplings 192 → 63); added async image decoding sitewide and removed unsolicited iOS install banners over opening copy.
  • Clearer lenses & inline theming: Working lenses now stay with you across pages, label their filter plainly (Hide other cards / Show every card), and pair with drag-scrubbable theme expressions on Settings and Composition. Discovery popups now match the page you are reading or stay silent.
  • Redesigned parser & live route: Rebuilt the Spw parser as a split desktop workbench with starter scenes (Character Beat, Site Card, System Transition) and theme-aware styling, and launched /tiktok/ for live viewers.
  • Accessibility & cleaner handoff: Named 344 folio/image links, fixed heading skips and form labels, restored screen-reader live announcements on section jumps, closed 60+ stray delimiters across 19 routes, and cut undeclared runtime root writes from 276 → 41 (multi-writer conflicts 11 → 2).
