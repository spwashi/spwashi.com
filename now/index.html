What This Funds

Not abstract creator support. Time for a repeatable practice: build, publish, inspect, reroute, and keep going. The real purchase is continuity — enough room for the cauldron to hold unfinished work without losing it.

  • Site updates that turn abstract ideas into usable public routes.
  • Local Proof Card development for asks, offers, pledges, references, and decisions.
  • Research and writing time that becomes clearer cards, lessons, methods, or services.
  • Captioned educational media, short-form videos, and grant-facing narrative work.
  • Hybridized curriculum experiments that let the learning travel farther than one room or schedule.
  • Coordination with collaborators across art, software, music, archives, learning, and care.
  • Basic stability while paid work is booked and delivered.
