## What the four found together

1. **The line is bone on every plane.** Each report found the same layer: what survives without the plane's own runtime.
   - **Physics:** `editLine` keeps every byte but the one place changed, a conservation law in the plain sense (a drag moved `~rain` between rooms whole).
   - **Quest:** a line on paper, held in an address.
   - **Ecology:** what reads with scripts off. On home that is 1,987 authored `data-spw-*` uses.
   - **Ground:** one string read three ways, as a card, as slots and as a field.

   Read as an accounting, each plane is bone plus boon plus bane:
   - **Ecology:** boon is what arrival grows (55,590 grown uses, 545 grown gesture contracts). Bane is what that growth costs: root recalc, 51% of a phone screen covered after one hold, and two answers to one touch.
   - **Physics:** boon is every pull (anchor, tie, center), bane every push (charge, collision, edge), and heat is the time either may act.

   This holds as an accounting on each plane. It does not hold as a map of lands onto sites. The lands live inside factshift.center's physics, as force profiles that all repel and differ in damping ([ground.spw](ground.spw), mappings m1). "Partly a reflection" is the right strength.
2. **The two lapsed lands were the transitions.** boon (arrival) and bone (hold) are rest states, and bane reads best as the cost a commit spends. bonk (held → taken) and honk (the receipt announced) are the lifecycle's two moves. They are the two lands that lapsed ([ground.spw](ground.spw), mappings).
3. **Readers disagree where the grammar should be shared.**
   - The dot is "one name" on /forms/ and two nodes on factshift.
   - `< >` is "what this is about" on the guide and "where it goes" on /forms/ and /pivot.
   - spwashi.com names three operators differently from spw.quest and factshift.center.
   - Three tables give each land a different sigil.
   - The workbench reads the pentad three ways: as material, as status, and as a slot named charge.

   Every report's first workbench ask is the same: one descriptor for operators, joins and valence (OPERATORS.md S1, grown).
4. **Answers the reader can't see, or two answers to one touch.** Each breaks the reward contract the way silence does:
   - one hold gets the cauldron and the region menu together;
   - lines carried to factshift land in a closed window;
   - operator motion is a two-second transient, so marks show only as offsets;
   - the Boonhonk Register's lede is hidden at default density.

   The contract should add: a touch resolves once, where it is seen ([ecology.spw](ecology.spw), proposals over_answer).
5. **The look carries the stage, and motion marks the change.** In the physics prototype, motion told open from settled and showed the moment of decision. It did not tell answered from let go; the fill and one taut tie did. That fits OPERATORS.md ("the look follows the stage"), and the attention research [physics.spw](physics.spw) cites. Lifecycle slices L1–L3 lean on the look and spend motion on transitions.

## What the material is worth

One expression is read three ways today: wording on a spwashi.com card, slots on spw.quest, relations on a factshift.center field. `#spw64=` carries it between them byte for byte.
- **For an academic reader:**
  - Where two readings part can be counted: one changed slot, every other byte kept.
  - A hold is a countable unit of attention. Home offers 523 things to hold, and a visit takes one or two.
  - /forms/ is a set of contrasting cases.
  - The ground report reads the three readings as levels of text comprehension (wording, propositions, situation model), the lineage Spw began in (2017). That is a reading to test (`wonder_ground_three_readings`), not a claim.
- **For a business reader:**
  - A brief is a line, and the slot where two stakeholders' lines part is the meeting's agenda.
  - A screenshot is both the receipt and the source.
  - The ecology probes make a short, repeatable audit for a client's site: which components claim one gesture, what covers reading text at rest, and how much arrival grows against what was authored.
- **Nothing here is a price or a promise.** The order card's wording and anything about Dregg stay the creator's.

## 2026-11-26 Audit reconciliation

Measured in [ecosystem-planes-pivots-ontologies](../../../.spw/audits/ecosystem-planes-pivots-ontologies-2026-11.spw) against the live repos and workbench theory (`charge-valence-brace-bridge.spw`, `cross-product-rederivation.spw`, `2026-11-11-one-table-many-scales.spw`):

1. **Effect grades sharpen the bone/boon/bane accounting.** Every plane now splits gesture effects into `L0` transient lean (0 diff on lift), `L1` session hold (local view/fold/aperture/pin, line untouched), and `L2` document write (`editLine` / URL / file). Keeping `L0` and `L1` distinct from `L2` prevents "every scrub writes the document" drift.
2. **Per-frame heat is already proven on Quest, not yet on Factshift.** `workers/spw-quest/src/physics.js` runs per-body warm/cool clocks so a visitor entering one body leaves settled siblings at `0.0 px` displacement; `factshift.center` still runs one global d3 `alpha` (plus continuous `entropyForce` kicks), leaking wakes across frames. Porting per-frame clocks (`factshift-center` P1) closes the largest physics gap between the two planes.
3. **Honest paper-vs-code boundary.** The workbench's `cross-product-rederivation.spw` separates four formal cognitive axes (`orientation`, `containment`, `charge`, `role`) on paper from what live DOM/canvas code actually wires. On `spwashi.com`, `data-spw-charge` still has three uncoordinated writers (`brace-gestures.js`, `haptics.js`, `brace-pivots.js`) and `brace:*` arming events do not feed `charge-field.js`.
4. **Five-way `charge` collision confirmed.** Per `charge-valence-brace-bridge.spw`, `charge` now collides across five distinct meanings: (a) grain aperture disclosure (`open`/`close`), (b) d3 repulsion `forces.charge`, (c) the `boon`/`bane`/`bone`/`bonk`/`honk` valence pentad, (d) upstream `ChargePacket` (`G6_discharge_homonym`), and (e) `spwashi.com`'s `charge-field.js` phase (`idle`/`primed`/`charged`/`sustained`). Disambiguation lands in `.spw/conventions/{word-sets,charge-cycle,semantic-braces}.spw`.
5. **Land sigil split remains live.** `factshift.center`'s `SITE_CONFIGS` maps `boon`/`bane`/`bone` to `* . #`, whereas `spwashi.com`'s domain pages map them to `> ! ^`. One upstream `operator_table` + valence descriptor (`week_2026_11_26`) stays the clean resolution.
