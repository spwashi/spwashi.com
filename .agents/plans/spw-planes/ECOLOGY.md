# Ecology plane: spwashi.com as a component ecology

2026-11-07 · one of four planes (ground, ecology, physics, quest) · read-only pass against https://spwashi.com
Stills: `design/components/captures/spw-planes-2026-11-07/ecology/` (gitignored). Probes and JSON: the session scratchpad, `planes-ecology/`.

**Headline.** Ecology does real work here in three places: competition for one niche, succession on arrival, and supply against uptake. Each one predicted something I then found. Exchange, mycorrhiza and decomposers are still metaphor, because no component gives anything up when another wins. The sharpest finding: one hold on a word at pocket gets two answers from three listening engines. The runtime's own competition sensor reads "clear" while the carry pieces sit over paragraphs and links.

## 2. The plane's theory as it stands

- **Seats.** "Name the seat (hook, hub, cluster, path, read, wide) before seating the widget. If a widget cannot name its region, it does not belong on the reader surface" (`.spw/conventions/region-component-ecology.spw:33`). The stack is posture → seat → pack → gravity → resonance → still (:38-46). Chrome is `chrome_field` and never pack-local (:91-94).
- **Biome.** Soil means 6,430 downward declarations against 197 `:has()`, 33:1 (`.spw/conventions/component-biome.spw:29-39`). Strategies: ruderal cards, networked cauldron and ledger (:63-71). Exchange must be "bidirectional and costly" (:74-79). Decomposers are missing (:81-95). Attention is the scarce thing (:108-115). Falsifiers: "an exchange that costs the giver nothing", "succession simulated with a timer" (:127-134).
- **Nutrient.** Attention is "conserved per visit" and "competed for", and "components compete for attention and never for custom properties" (`.spw/conventions/attention-field.spw:89-99`).
- **Five extensions.** Return, allometry, competitive exclusion, mycorrhiza and seral succession (`.spw/caches/component-ecology-theory-2026-11.spw:29-107`). Its own test: a metaphor "earns its keep when it predicts a bug or a cost before a screenshot or profiler catches it" (:130).
- **Touch.** Phase ladder idle → approach → prime → charge → inspect → discover → settle (`.spw/conventions/interaction-microstates.spw:55-69`). "The finger's target and the motion's meaning are the same object" (:89-97). Reward contract: "never silent absorption" (:99-110).
- **Chrome.** The theory as run has one ladder, slots no CSS reads, and four pocket conditions (`.spw/caches/chrome-ecology-2026-11.spw:57-66`). The carry corner should be one composition (:81). One hold runs on two clocks (:117; `interaction-grammar/PLAN.md:17`, slice 4 at :24).
- **Plans.** Each pocket frame gets one extra dense object (`component-region-personality/PLAN.md:100`). Hosts write their line and none reads it back (`brace-semantics/PLAN.md:93-102`), and authored hosts are piloted with the instrument (:143). The runtime defines zero custom elements (`ecosystem-architecture/PLAN.md:29`). 352 root selectors × unguarded writes means "every redundant `html` dataset write invalidates style for the document" (`runtime-plane-consolidation/PLAN.md:70`).
- **Creator notes (memory).** Reading, then interaction, then serialization. Modules as authored hosts with state on the host (2026-10-06). Folios as soil, and "soil" stays off the route until two series exist.

## 3. Where theory and surface disagree

1. **The hold has three tenants, not two.** The plans count haptics (420 ms coarse, `interface/haptics.js:126`) and brace-gestures (420 ms, `brace-gestures.js:72`). `region-menu.js` also arms a hold, at 560/460/360 ms (:49-53), on any `[data-spw-concept]` or `[data-spw-semantic-expression]` (:1160-1166). Living terms carry `data-spw-concept`, and the runtime writes expressions onto 238 more home elements. Time does not separate them, because a deliberate hold outlasts both thresholds. Receipts 4, 5, 7 and 11 show one hold getting two answers.
2. **The reward contract forbids silence but allows over-answering.** No clause says a touch resolves once (`interaction-microstates.spw:99-110`). The host's contract string names one answer (receipt 11).
3. **The runtime watches the wrong competitor.** `measureFloatingChromeOcclusion` (`kernel/dom-contracts.js:1153`) compares open chrome with open chrome. It never compares chrome with the reading ground, so it reads clear while text and links sit underneath (receipt 14). The seat rule (:33) exempts chrome and gives it no rule about where it may rest.
4. **The pioneer stage is not quiet.** cet-005 says a cold visit gets "zero ambient ornament" (`component-ecology-theory-2026-11.spw:105`). A first visit at phone gets the install hint (`runtime/shell/pwa-update-handler.js:438-444`). Headless Chrome with touch on a Mac passes its iOS Safari check, so an iPhone's first visit sees the same thing.
5. **Grown outweighs authored.** Theory calls the authored attributes the contract and the profiler output "receipts, not a second essay" (`region-component-ecology.spw:62, :65`). Arrival grows 29× the authored data-spw uses. 545 of 549 contracts and 238 of 310 expressions are grown, and grown contracts are what a finger meets.
6. **Supply is reported, uptake is not.** The cauldron is the one networked organism a reader sees, and it tells them "524 on this page can be held". A visit takes one or two. A brace hold on home gathered "home", the subject every home expression shares, so likely many hosts gather the same word.
7. **Two state ladders.** The creator's interaction-contract note runs idle → preview → active → sustained → emitted → settled. The stable convention runs the ladder in item 2 of the theory list. The seed card shows a third shape, "saved" on every keystroke.

## 4. Proposals

**(a) Theory**
- a1. Call ecology a model where it can be tested and a metaphor everywhere else. Three laws, each with a probe:
  - **Niche:** one answer per gesture per host. Count the tenants of each gesture.
  - **Ground:** at rest, chrome may cover chrome or empty ground, never reading text or a control.
  - **Succession:** arrival grows, and growth on `<html>` is paid for by every element.
  Mark exchange and decomposers as `prime` until some component gives something up. Owner: `component-biome.spw`.
- a2. Add over-answering to the reward contract. A touch resolves once, in one place, and two answers to one touch break the contract the same way silence does. Owner: `interaction-microstates.spw#reward_contract`.
- a3. Use bone, boon and bane as the plane's accounting. Bone is what reads with scripts off (1,987 attributes, 356 links). Boon is what arrival grows (55,590 attributes, 545 contracts, the cauldron, the lens). Bane is what that growth costs (root recalc, 51% of the screen covered, double answers). Each slice names the layer it moves. This is the creator's call.
- a4. Gate the seral stages by visit, not by timer. Install hints and promos belong to a returning visit (cet-005's climax stage).

**(b) Interactivity** (each one ends at a browser demo on a coarse pointer)
- b1. The region menu yields a hold to the cauldron on `.spw-living-term`, `[data-spw-living-term]` and `[data-spw-cauldron-candidate="true"]`, the way brace-gestures yielded on 2026-09-03. A double tap or a sigil still opens it. Verify: a held term at pocket gathers, and the menu stays shut.
- b2. Give interaction-grammar slice 1 ("Do this, expect this") a row for the region menu's hold as the third clock.
- b3. Read chrome-over-ground as a sensor first, with no change to paint. Add the probe from receipt 14 beside `measureFloatingChromeOcclusion`, as a receipt only.
- b4. The install hint waits for a second visit or for settle (`pwa-update-handler.js:405-445`).

**(c) Public copy** (current → proposed)
- `public/js/interface/composition.js:1437`: "Primed deep-link fragment gathered from ${originLabel}: ${label}." → "Held: ${label}. Hold one more and the two make a mix." This echoes `_partials/site-footer.html:59`, "Press and hold a highlighted word to keep it here. Two make a mix."
- `public/js/interface/composition.js:720`: " ${count} on this page can be held." → " ${count} more on this page will keep."
- `public/js/runtime/regions/region-menu.js:688` and `:693` fallbacks: "semantic region" → "this passage", and "region actions" → "what a hold can do".
- `index.html:123` kicker above "I'm Spwashi.": "home page entry" → "front room". The page already speaks in rooms ("Rooms on this page", "Next room").

**(d) Workbench asks** (recorded here only)
- d1. Gesture contracts are free text (`tap:inspect hold:prime-to-cauldron`). Could the canon read a contract as an expression, such as `hold[prime]{cauldron}`, so that "one answer per host" can be parsed and checked?
- d2. A concrete case for brace-semantics slice 5 (label lexing): every home brace gathers "home". A labeled pair such as `{_why notice }_why` would give each host its own handle in the cauldron.

## 5. Material and value

**Made of.** Bone is authored HTML, CSS and copy that read with scripts off. That includes the cauldron's own form in the footer (receipt 12). Boon is what arrival grows: 67 modules, 255 attributes on `<html>`, contracts on 549 hosts.

Operators sit in components as state, not decoration:
- Lens seats print their sigil: `.reading ^systems ~play @studio` (`index.html:199-202`).
- The cauldron chip's prefix is its phase: `.` resting, `~` held, `*` ready, `^` mixed (`interface/cauldron/live-expression.js:23-26`).
- The order card marks each field: `> PIECE ^ FORM ~ STORY @ RANGE ~ RELAY`.
- Home authors 24 operator strings, and 39 aliases fold them into one table (`kernel/operator-detection.js:321-368`).

**Behaves.** Succession takes about 11 s on a phone. Gestures are contested (receipts 4–7, 11). The ground is shared and costly: every root write restyles against the 352 root selectors.

**Academic reader.** A hold is a countable unit of attention. It is one deliberate act, with a time constant (420–560 ms) and a destination. Home offers 523 sources and a visit takes a few. That ratio is the attention field's "nutrient" reading made countable.

Spw began in a 2017 Learning-from-Text course, as a way to find where two readers' understandings stop sharing ground. Here the lens makes a reader's chosen ground explicit (`home[systems]`), and the cauldron line records what they took. Two readers' lines can be compared slot by slot, the way spw.quest compares specimens. The site counts events and never knows who made them (`interaction-microstates.spw`, wonder 2).

**Business reader.**
- **Audit.** For a client's site, the plane gives a short audit that can be repeated: which components claim the same gesture, what covers reading text at rest, and what arrival grows against what was authored. Each finding comes with a still and a count, as in section 1.
- **A&R and services.** The folio card shows a catalogue where a screenshot of a filled card is the order, written as Spw a person can keep.

No outcome or price is implied. The wording of the order card is the creator's (financial, so confirm).
