# Plan: Team Copy Reading & Production Orientation
#
# A guide for reading the site's copy collaboratively to orient production 
# during seasons of family and relationship focus.

## Public Goal
To orient team production around the core human-first values, narratives, and structural metaphors of spwashi.com. As collaborators begin prioritizing their personal lives, relationships, and families, the site's own copy serves as a structural blueprint for sustainable, high-quality craft that honors pacing, boundaries, and compound practice rather than high-friction sprints.

We read the copy not to memorize biography, but to establish a shared language for what we build: treating software as an editorial kitchen, code as punctuation, and version history as sedimented tending.

## Suggested Reading Strata & Orientation Prompts

To align production with family-centric life, focus your team readings around these three primary themes:

### 1. Bounded Systems and Sustainable Cadence
*   **Copy Focus**: Read the Homepage and About page sections on the **13-step release cycles** (closing A and B cycles on the 13th and 26th), the **200-day chunk epochs**, and the **stable kernel vs. mutating lattice**.
*   **Orientation Prompt**:
    > "How do we define our own production 'kernel'—the few things that must stay stable and bounded so that our daily life with our families remains peaceful, while allowing our collaborative work (the 'lattice') to mutate at its own natural pace?"
*   **System Value**: We value continuous, low-friction practice over big-bang releases. The site's rhythm is a buffer against burnout.

### 2. The Kitchen Metaphor: Mise en Place and Byproduct Recovery
*   **Copy Focus**: Read the **Recipes** index (`recipes/index.html`) and the **Fermentation** page (`recipes/fermentation/index.html`).
*   **Orientation Prompt**:
    > "If fermentation is 'producing conditions, not outcomes,' how do we inoculate our workspaces to support slow, healthy emergence? When we work in our local cells, are we maintaining a clean 'mise en place' so that others can pick up the work without cognitive overhead?"
*   **System Value**: Documentation, clean files, and clear ontology definitions are how we respect each other's time and attention. When a collaborator must step away for family, a clean station makes succession painless.

### 3. Progressive Whimsy and Restraint
*   **Copy Focus**: Read the **Design Whimsy** conventions (`.spw/conventions/design-whimsy.spw`) and the **Attention Architecture** specifications.
*   **Orientation Prompt**:
    > "Are we honoring the 'calm at rest, delight on contact' principle in our work? How can our production interface stay quiet and unobtrusive, yet reward sustained interest?"
*   **System Value**: We prioritize human attention. Visual noise is a tax; gentle resonance is an invitation.

## Action Plan for the Reading Session
1.  **Dequantize the Text**: Have the team read the compiled markdown document. Focus on the tone of the narrator: *"I build software and make art. I want the site to work for adults still learning in the middle of ordinary life."*
2.  **Locate the Seams**: Identify where the prose directly intersects with the code surfaces (e.g. how a paragraph's "temper" or "bloom" maps to a CSS operator timing, or how "Grounding Rod" maps to interaction context).
3.  **Draft a Succession Pact**: Agree on local file boundaries, using the new `modular-experience-slices` tending notes to deposit learnings, allowing people to transition smoothly between active tending and family presence.
