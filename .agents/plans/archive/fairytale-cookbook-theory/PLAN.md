# Fairytale Cookbook Theory

## Goal

Make spwashi.com feel more like a fairytale guide into itself: an academic-adjacent living cookbook for software engineers, parents, and returning learners who want to stay intellectually sharp while developing practical theory, family nutrition intuition, and story-rich mnemonic practice.

## Editorial Contract

- Treat culinary techniques as useful operations, not decorative metaphors.
- Let technique names imply shopping lists, mise en place, family meal planning, character mnemonics, and scene seeds.
- Let batch bases and byproducts imply provisions, storage, distribution, recovery, and small realms.
- Keep visible interaction language closer to field-guide handles than generic UI inventory while preserving native accessibility semantics.
- Keep the tone clear enough for an academic book and strange enough for a guidebook that can lead into RPG Wednesday.
- Make fantasy and romance practical: ways to keep attention, care, humor, and relationship fresh through repeatable scenes.

## Route Targets

- Home: explain the site as a readable guide for adults who may return to formal learning later.
- Recipes: make shopping lists, technique clusters, family nutrition, and mnemonic characters explicit.
- Software: connect parsing, projection, and invariants to cookbook operations and repeatable learning.
- Design: make culinary instruction a component design method with visible handoff into scenes and prompts.

## Follow-Up

- Add processed images for pantry, garden, family-table, and theory-map motifs.
- Consider a `.spw` recipe/operator glossary when the technique vocabulary stabilizes.
- Add settings/query links for copy depth: concise, academic, fairytale, kitchen, and runtime.
