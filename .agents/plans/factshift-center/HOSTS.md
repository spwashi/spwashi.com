## A lean that expires

`ephemeral_bias` in `.spw/_workbench/docs/theory/spw/depiction-frames.spw` is a lean toward something, for a while, and then gone unless kept.

The hash note and the handle note share one hold, `--ephemeral-hold` (2.4s), read by `workers/factshift-center/app/src/js/ui/ephemeral.ts`. A flick whose move is taken back, and a proximity ring that means a release would connect, are the same kind of lean. A primed joint waits for a second gesture. It does not expire on that clock.

## The document cursor

The lines this browser held are one place: `src/js/ui/document-place.ts`, key `factshift.lines`. A swipe on the hash or on the held-line row asks that place where it is, then steps. The step does not rewrite a line.

Concept history (`ui/concept-history.ts`) stays the walk between concepts in the address. Frames (`stage/frame.ts`) stay pages kept under the hash. Those are other times.

## The hand

| hand | what it does | the line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| slide | ← → on the hash or the held-line row | stays |
| lean | a drag inside an ordered body, or a proximity ring | stays, and the seat or the target is marked |
| keep | the finger lifts on that seat, on a link target, or inside a container | `editLine` or `linkByDrop` writes |

A physics collision (`forces.ts`, force `collide`) shoves. It does not write. A slide never rewrites the line.

While a part is inside its ordered body, the seat a release would take wears `data-line-lean="slot"` (`anatomy-drop.ts` `leanSeat`). Leaving the body clears the mark. The write is the drop that was already there. The mark follows a line that reads whole. A line that reads only in part still writes on release, and stays quiet while the hand moves.
