Shareable B2B Summary

proof

Use this block when someone needs a straightforward description they can forward around a team, founder group, or investor thread.

Spwashi helps teams turn blurry work into named structure: clearer UX flows, stronger architecture notes, reusable vocabulary, more durable public surfaces, and proof artifacts that make the next decision easier to share. The useful part is not just the endpoint; it is making the in-between legible enough to trust.

What this business does

Builds readable software and media systems: scoped product work, UX-flow testing, architecture memos, creator surfaces, semantic spell recipes, and shareable proof artifacts that help a team explain itself. It also helps when the work is still in motion and needs a surface that can hold the draft without hiding it.

Why it matters long-term

Strong codebases fail when memory, timing, and communication decay. Better literacy around contracts, handoff, checkpoints, and public reasoning lowers that risk. That makes businesses more stable and leaves more room for schools, parents, and future readers to benefit from the work.

What makes the network useful

Spwashi is the coordinating ecosystem brand: a growing network of engineers and agentic pools, with different gradients of initiative and capacity. Individual creators keep differentiating and developing their own unique skill combinations; author-facing and worldbuilding sensibilities help those combinations meet in morale, alignment, and durable collaboration.

Four Paths

Each panel is a short summary and a link. No page tries to do everything on its own.

Creator Work

For authors, illustrators, and independent makers. Cover design, illustration, interactive book covers, ebook formatting, and a public series home when the story needs a place to live. If you want editing or a co-writer alongside a genre, I likely know the right person.

Bookmark this for an author friend, a visual collaborator, or a developer who needs a humane entry point into publishing work.

Product & A&R

For artists, labels, studios, and product teams deciding what to make next and who it is for. I've worked in media since college and keep relationships across music, film, illustration, and software, so a project can find its collaborators and its first real audience without buying either. Scoping, roadmaps, repertoire, release plans, and the introductions that make them move. Right now that includes marketing a feature-length film due in 2027; its season promo can be backed.

Bookmark this for an artist between releases, a founder whose product works before anyone has found it, or a studio choosing its next slate.

Systems Work

For businesses, microbusinesses, and investor-visible teams. Web development, custom applications, consulting, architecture advisory, and QA through experienced agentic pools. Custom and hybrid quotes on request, especially when the work needs to outlast a single launch cycle.

Production code since 2013, including web development for University of Illinois Extension's Community Economic Development team and senior frontend work. Rates reflect that engineering time and the art and business practice around it: getting a less experienced hand running in your own style usually costs more than bringing in someone who already has one.

Bookmark this for an owner, operator, or technical lead who wants a calm second opinion on the road ahead.

Ecosystem

Engineers, entrepreneurs, authors, editors, illustrators, librarians, gardeners. If you want to join a group or want me to pass a project along, this is the place. It also helps a student or early-career visitor see that careers are often adjacent before they are linear.

Referrals shouldn't end the thought — the page stays after the introduction, with links into the work that follows.

Care Interface

Software engineering can be isolating, and therapy can fail when the shared language is missing. Start with the care interface for translation cards, engineer-friendly prompts, peer witness structures, and safety boundaries. Then use the care practices page when you want the local intake form.

For anyone trying to turn private loops into safer language, community, and next steps.

Four Economies Reasonable alignment across roles

                    

                        

                            Software Engineers
                            
You value verified leverage over typed hours: deterministic lint/contract harnesses, readable specs, edge workers, and keeping public tools open. Start with a $12–$30/mo Studio Seat to back shared infrastructure, or book a $250–$750 Teardown or $1,000–$2,400 One-Close Micro-Sprint when AI velocity creates verification debt.

                        

                        

                            People Who Lead
                            
You value lower coordination load and honest risk signaling without taking on a $180k+ full-time hire prematurely. Anchor decisions to the 13th/26th cadence through Architecture Memos or Light / Core / Embedded Retainers ($1,200–$4,500+/mo) and sliding Org advisory.

                        

                        

                            Entrepreneurs
                            
You value speed-to-signal without locking capital into speculative multi-month proposals. Validate first with a 30-day credited Teardown, ship a flagship wedge in a One-Close Micro-Sprint ($1,000–$2,400), and pay per close with explicit stop points.

                        

                        

                            Artists
                            
You value autonomy, tactile craft, and humane pacing without studio gatekeeping. Use Indie sliding rates ($110–$140/hr or $250 Teardown), build modularly by close ($250 plate to $2,500 author home), share annual model costs through Studio Seats, or order laminated folios by their ecosystem role.

                    #>cadence
                    
Release Cadence

                

                
Release days follow a steady twice-monthly cadence: the 13th and 26th of each month. The A cycle closes on the 13th; the B cycle closes on the 26th. Twenty-four releases a year — one step more than nothing. The deeper modular rhythm powers the numericity system.

                
If you're starting a project and don't know how to pace it, you're welcome to borrow the rhythm. Two intentional steps a month is modest and cumulative. I ship on those days whether or not anyone is watching, which means contracts anchored to them have real landmarks.
