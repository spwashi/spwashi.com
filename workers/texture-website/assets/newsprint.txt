newsprint (texture.website/newsprint), paper
Plant fibres felted into a sheet; how long the fibres are and how the sheet is pressed decide its tooth, its translucency and how it takes ink.
Qualities to try: deckled edges, visible fibre, soft tooth, faint ruling, foxing

As a tile:
  seamless repeating texture of newsprint, close view, soft overcast light, deckled edges, visible fibre --tile --ar 1:1 --no text, letters, logo, watermark
As a backdrop:

With another material:
