Materials

  • Paper fiber grain

    Cellulose fibres felted into a sheet as water drains through a screen; a deckled edge is where the pulp thinned against the mould's frame.

    image · recipe · line

  • Linen weave cloth

    Flax bast fibre in plain weave: each weft thread passes over one warp and under the next, the simplest interlacing that holds.

    image · recipe · line

  • Harlequin diamonds pattern

    A rhombic tiling: one diamond, translated along two directions, covers the plane with no gaps and no overlaps.

    image · recipe · line

  • Cream ochre wash wash

    Iron-oxide pigment suspended in water; as a wash dries, particles carried to its edges leave them darker, the coffee-ring effect.

    image · recipe · line

  • Lattice plate plate

    A truss carries load through triangles, the one polygon whose shape is fixed by the lengths of its sides.

    image · recipe · line

  • Bone plate plate

    Bone is a composite: stiff mineral crystals in a tough collagen matrix, strong where either alone would crack or bend.

    image · recipe · line

  • Flow plate plate

    Gears pass motion by meshing teeth; the ratio of their tooth counts is the ratio of their turns.

    image · recipe · line

  • Grid simple

    A square lattice: two families of parallel lines at right angles, the plane cut into equal cells.

    image · recipe · line

  • Dot screen simple

    A halftone prints a grey as dots of one ink; the share of the cell a dot covers is the tone the eye sees.

    image · recipe · line

  • Hatch simple

    Hatching shades with parallel lines: closer lines read darker, and crossing a second set deepens it again.

    image · recipe · line

  • Stripes simple

    A periodic pattern of one interval: shift it by its period and it lands on itself.

    image · recipe · line

  • Checker simple

    Two colourings of a square grid where no cell shares an edge with its own colour: the board's two-colour rule.

    image · recipe · line

  • Ramp simple

    A linear ramp interpolates between two values; the eye reads equal steps of lightness as equal, not equal steps of light.

    image · recipe · line
