About#

Spw started while I was exploring factshift, before it became factshift.com and then factshift.center.

It was first meant as a tagging system for pivoting across higher dimensions. Then I learned more about cognition and epistemology. Now Spw is a language for navigating the metamaterial properties of linguistics.

In practice, a mark says what a word is doing and a brace says which slot it fills. Hold two readings on the shelf and name the slot where they part, or change one slot in the editor. Joins stay distinct, so a comparison can say which relation moved.

  • . A dot makes one name.
  • , A comma holds a company.
  • ; A semicolon keeps an order.
  • ~> A path.

The fuller statement is what Spw should let you say.
