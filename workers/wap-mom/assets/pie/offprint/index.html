Pie

The crust is a proof.

A pie is a circle you are allowed to cut.

Everything about it is measured before it is baked: the flour, the fat, the heat, the time. The oven is the only argument that convinces a room.

A pie that waits for the knife is a theorem with a crust. π never ends. Pie ends when it is shared.

Uses

In the hand
Moving a 9-inch recipe into a 10-inch tin? The bigger tin holds about a quarter more, so scale the filling up by a quarter.
At the table
The first slice always comes out untidy. Serve it to the baker.
At work
A recipe is a claim anyone with an oven can test. Write methods down so someone else could bake them.
In the mind
π cannot be finished. A pie has to be. Most good things are one or the other.
