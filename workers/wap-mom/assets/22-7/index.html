22/7

Close enough to share.

Twenty-two divided by seven is 3.142857, repeating. It is a little too big, off by about one part in 2,500.

Over two thousand years ago, Archimedes trapped π between two shapes with ninety-six sides each, one just inside a circle and one just outside. 22/7 was the outside edge of the trap, and it is the one people kept.

Close is not cheap. Close is hospitality.

π

Uses

In the hand
A 7-inch plate is 22 inches around, near enough. Multiply a width by 22, divide by 7, and you are within a hair.
At the table
An answer someone can use now is often kinder than an exact one that arrives late.
At work
Before chasing precision, say how close is close enough. One part in 2,500 has served for two thousand years.
In the mind
Archimedes knew his answer was wrong, and exactly how wrong. That is its own kind of knowing.
