factshift.center

I'm Spwashi. I build software and make art. factshift.com is the prototype: a force simulation I built in a burst at the end of 2023 and settled in October 2024, where a line of Spw can be written and moved. factshift.center is the examination that follows. It asks which concepts a particular set of physics can represent.

The difference

People hesitate to engage with AI. I understand that. As an engineer I can't really mind it. factshift.center extends factshift.com through agentic development, adding depth to the cohesion and the performance: the concepts hold together across the line, the canvas, and a still, and the forces do work the line already named.

A line is the specimen. The word sits where the reading begins. The body holds its parts. A ( ) applies to the word together with that body. A concept refers to the whole reading. A join is the pull between two claims. What the physics can represent is whatever still reads as the line after you move it. A picture of the canvas and the source stay one object, including for someone who arrives through a screenshot.
