Domains

Each domain is a door with its own job. Some carry the language, Spw, and the tools that read it. Some carry stories and a periodical. One carries art and its shop. They link to each other on purpose, so a line written on one can be read, taken apart, or laid out on another.

^"language_and_tools"

The language and its tools

spw.quest

The guide to Spw. Learn it a mark at a time, take a line apart and put it back together, and set the workbench up in a repository with one block a person or an agent can run.

Visit spw.quest

factshift.center

A canvas for one line of Spw. It lays the line out as nodes and regions you can move, link and rewrite, and asks which concepts a particular set of physics can represent.

Visit factshift.center

factshift.com

The prototype. It began as a better Wikipedia and turned toward learning: short claims, reframings, and the first Spw, a way to write a claim from more than one perspective.

Visit factshift.com

^"land_system"

The land system

Three fields that only make sense together: one that draws in, one that pushes away, and the medium both act through. The cluster register on the about page holds them as a set.

boon.land

The positive field: arrival, benefit and growth, in tension with bane.

bane.land

The negative field: departure, cost and boundary, in tension with boon.

bone.land

The structural carrier: the neutral field where boon and bane act. It takes no side, and makes sides possible.

Between them

A line of Spw can travel: from a page here into spw.quest's editor, or onto factshift.center's canvas, and back by its address. How much should travel with it, where its name comes from, and which reader sets the standard when two disagree are still open. Those questions are kept on the website design page.
