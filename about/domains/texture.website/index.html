texture.website

texture.website builds a prompt from its own address. /sea-glass/~frost-ferns reads as sea glass drifting through frost ferns: each word a material, bottom first, and the mark before a word says how it meets the one beneath.

Light, wear, process, scale and scene are ladders of plain words, so a setting reads back as the phrase it adds: raking dawn, worn, cyanotype, close. Ninety-one materials each carry a class, with its tactile qualities and a note on why that kind of matter looks the way it does; any other word still works and is said as written. Each listed material has a seamless tile at its own address, /terrazzo.png, open to any origin, to hand an image model as an image prompt or a style reference.

Role

A place to learn prompting by changing one thing. Each pivot names the Midjourney technique it teaches, with its source: a ladder written as a permutation, materials weighted against each other, chaos, a random style reference, the layers reversed. What you have tried stays in your browser and can be forgotten.

Relation

Three views look through other eyes: Minimalist@, PromptEngineer@ and StoryDesigner@. Every composition is also a line of Spw that factshift.center lays out as nodes, and the Midjourney bench here keeps the reference packets those prompts feed.
