A small team can become an economy when the work has clear roles, records, and circulation.

Coordination means turning chat, attention, and ideas into artifacts people can point at, fund, improve, preserve, or ship. The point is not to end the conversation; it is to give the conversation a shape it can keep moving through.

Use this when

interest needs to become roles, records, funded work, release rhythm, or a shared next action. A good handoff keeps the current process visible.

What it protects

the difference between chat momentum, actual commitment, revisable agreement, and shipped artifact. Midprocess states are where that difference becomes legible.

What it can become

a consensus card, work sprint, public request, membership path, research brief, or RPG production loop. Each of those can be screenshot, shared, and carried forward.

Coordination Rule

Every project should produce one of four things, but the route there is usually messier than the final object suggests:

  1. A public artifact.
  2. A useful record.
  3. A funded offer.
  4. A reusable method.

@growth_loop

Attention To Coordination

  1. Publish an insight, ask, offer, or proposal.
  2. Turn it into a Local Proof Card.
  3. Screenshot it.
  4. Share it to TikTok, group chat, DMs, or notes.
  5. Link back to /now/ or /cards/.
  6. Convert interest into support, membership, commission, or collaboration.
  7. Archive the card as a reference.
  8. Keep a screenshot of the midprocess state that taught you something.

Coordination exits

Follow the route that matches the next kind of evidence the work needs. If the evidence is still forming, stay with the process long enough to read it clearly.

  • Consensus cardsTurn a proposal into a visible agreement state.
  • ServicesTranslate coordination into a practical offer or commission.
  • ResearchGive a decision source language and evidence.
  • Runtime auditInspect which scripts and settings change behavior.
