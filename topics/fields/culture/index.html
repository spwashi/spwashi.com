#>ways

What a tradition does

Six ways, the same six this site uses for its holidays and its writing. A tradition can do more than one.

Regard

Looking from a position, and holding something in a light: a mask, a costume, a name taken for a day. What it changes can stay.

the mask, on Holidays

Gather

Bringing people and parts to one table: a shared meal, a potluck, a seat kept for someone.

recipes as social memory

Give

Letting something made or chosen be held by someone else: a gift, an offering, a recipe passed on.

the gift season, on Holidays

Cross

Marking a threshold: a new year, a first day, a vow said out loud and returned to later.

the new year seed card

Keep time

Holding a rhythm together: a weekly game, a market day, the close of a season.

RPG Wednesday, every week

Remember

Keeping what passed: an anniversary, a name said again, a dish made the way someone made it.
