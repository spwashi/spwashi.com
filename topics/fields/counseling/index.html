Scope, Methods, and the Right Room

Licensed counselors work within a scope of practice, and so does this site: it can talk about attention and learning, and it cannot treat anyone. Two methods inform how it speaks. Cognitive behavioral therapy tests the thoughts that drive feelings. Motivational interviewing draws out a person’s own reasons for change. And when you need more than a page, 988 is the right room: call or text 988 in the U.S. Licensed counselors are among the family members who have reviewed my laminated folios as the process has developed since 2024.

Cognitive behavioral therapy

Motivational interviewing

#>parallel_theories

Parallel and Competing Theories

Parallel models of care from Zimbabwe and India. One extended talk therapy beyond the clinic; the other supplied roots that mindfulness-based therapies now draw on.

The Friendship Bench

In Zimbabwe, trained lay health workers, often grandmothers, offer structured problem-solving talk on park benches; a clinical trial found it reduced depression symptoms.

wiki: Friendship Bench

Vipassana

An Indian meditation tradition of observing sensation and thought without reacting, one of the roots modern mindfulness-based therapies draw on.

wiki: vipassana

At the table

A pot is company. It is not treatment. The Friendship Bench works because it is a specific method with trained lay workers, not because tea is therapy. At this table the playable constraint is the limit: one pot, a clock, and a sentence that nobody here is the clinician. If the scene needs a real line, it leaves the game. In the U.S. that line is 988. The site page for the boundary is mental health. The meal pattern on RPG Wednesday still holds: etiquette, debt, care, refusal.

Scene: one pot, a clock, and a rule spoken out loud. Care can be played. Diagnosis cannot.
