Principles From Neighboring Curricula

These are curricula I did not finish, or did not start: a peer's, a mentor's, or a family member's. Each page names the principles that curriculum teaches, cites a source, and links the topic on this site where that principle is already in use. Start with educational psychology if you want where Spw began, or industrial engineering if you want the decision tools.

Regional lenses

The principles above lean Western. These lenses follow parallel and competing theories from elsewhere, and how they shaped the infrastructure I work inside.

  • China, India, and the U.S.Universities, student flows, and competing digital rails.
  • Africa, Europe, and AustraliaSocial insurance, folk high schools, universal care, and reconciliation.
  • Central and South AmericaPublic murals, youth orchestras, a theatre of rehearsal, and an independent zero.

Biochemistry is the same kind of page for a subject rather than a person: ATP as the spent currency, redox as electron fall, and metabolism as a graph. Andragogy names Knowles's six assumptions for the adults in these rooms.
