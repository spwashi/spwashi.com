Layers, Systems, and Sequences

Geology reads the history of the Earth from rock. Superposition says older layers lie beneath younger ones, so depth is a clock. A petroleum system needs a source rock, a reservoir, a seal, and a trap, in the right order. And the research about all of it follows a filing sequence: I processed and filed petroleum research for the Illinois State Geological Survey, and following documents through that sequence taught me more about protocols than any course did.

Parallel and Competing Theories

Readings of the Earth from eleventh- and second-century China, reached independently and centuries before the European formulations.

Zhang Heng’s seismoscope

In 132 CE, Zhang Heng built a bronze vessel that signaled the direction of a distant earthquake by dropping a ball from a dragon’s mouth.

Shen Kuo on landforms

In the eleventh century, Shen Kuo reasoned from marine shells in mountain cliffs that the land had formed from deposited sediment and later uplift.
