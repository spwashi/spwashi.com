The Grammar of Proof

Logic is the grammar underneath every other page here. A proposition is a statement that is true or false; connectives (and, or, not, implies) build larger ones; quantifiers say "for every" and "there exists." Sets collect things, and a function pairs each input with exactly one output. Proof is how a claim earns its place: by showing it follows from what was already accepted.

#>parallel_theories

Parallel and Competing Theories

Formal reasoning was developed in parallel in India and China, with different questions at the center.

Nyāya

The Indian Nyāya school built a theory of valid knowledge and a five-part inference, with an example as a required step, and argued carefully about how we come to know.

wiki: Nyāya

Mohist logic

The Chinese Mohists studied names, distinctions, and argument by analogy, asking when two things may be called by the same name.

wiki: Mohism
