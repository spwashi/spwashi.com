Contract

In hand

Overlay or compare fields. Both sources stay readable in the result.

What it means

The mark is a relation, a third thing that lasts only while both fields are still visible. Where they disagree stays part of the relation.

Brace Physics

The brace carries both incoming fields. Agreement and disagreement stay inside the same boundary.

Before and after

Prefix &x primes a unit for integration. Postfix x& marks that the unit remains mergeable after reading.
