! Pragma

Pragma uses the same commit mark as other actions, !. It names one constraint the surface obeys.

Contract

In hand

Apply or inspect one named constraint. Neighboring surfaces stay as they were, and the constraint can be read before it acts.

What it means

The mark is force with a reason. The reason stays readable on the boundary while the force is still held.

Brace Physics

The brace holds one named force. The reason sits on the boundary, and the force stays inside that scope.

Before and after

Prefix !x applies force before contact. Postfix x! marks a discharged force or constraint after contact.
