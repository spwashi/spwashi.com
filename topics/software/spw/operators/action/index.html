Contract

In hand

Commit a local action or a projection. When the commit is unavailable, the missing precondition stays stated, and the page can show what a completed commit changed.

What it means

The mark is attention taking a position, force that stays accountable to the current frame. The intention stays readable in the moment before the consequence lands.

Brace Physics

The brace discharges. The release is visible as navigation, a projection, or a changed register.

Before and after

Prefix !x primes the next unit as a committing move. Postfix x! marks that the unit has been acted through. Use @x only when you mean perspective — situating the viewpoint, not forcing consequence.
