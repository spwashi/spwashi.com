Contract

In hand

Open a topical field and carry it into a later action, mode, or direction. The territory stays available for the next move.

What it means

The mark is an aperture, the opening through which a relation enters, before anything is rendered. A later move can still belong to the territory this opening named.

Brace Physics

The brace holds one capsule. What is inside shares a territory. The surface edge can close it.

Before and after

Prefix <x opens the field. The matching > projects that field outward. Opening and projecting are a pair.
