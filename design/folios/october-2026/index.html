Design / Folio Archive / October

October, off the scanner.

I scanned on five afternoons in late October. A hundred and fourteen new pieces hang here: folios, panels, cutouts moved around a clear sheet, and pages from my scratchpads.

They hang here in scan order, as September's do on the folio shelf. Open one to see it whole and read the question it asks. A note you leave stays in this browser.

Some September pieces went back through the scanner and keep their place on the folio shelf. Movies are emotions came back with a card taped on, dated October 04, so it hangs here as well.
