Component Options / Spacing

Density & Measure

Spacing scales smoothly when driven by density attributes rather than hard-coded inline values.

Research note (for interns + designers): "rich" / high-density often compelling in business/inspection/tool contexts (high information per inch, registers, settings lists). "minimal" + roomy more useful for experiential/relationship reading (breathing room, calm presence). Use data-spw-density + data-spw-locality + data-spw-physics-reason in your own components for granular, vocabulary-building architecture. See /design/ bench and .spw page_architecture_vocabulary.

Density: dense

High information per inch. Pads tightly and condenses line-height. Great for data registers, lists of settings, or tightly packed chips.

Density: soft

The default cadence. Provides comfortable breathing room for prose and general article blocks. Balances text measure against frame width.

Density: roomy

Expansive bounds for hero blocks, orientation frames, and introductory material. Creates a moment of pause inside the scroll.

Reading: measure

Caps the width to roughly 68ch (characters) for ideal reading of long-form prose, avoiding eye fatigue on wide screens.
