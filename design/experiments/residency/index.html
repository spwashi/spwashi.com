Charter of the season residency

Demo. A draft charter, written to see how a residency could read. Nothing on this page is announced, scheduled, or promised.

For the production season, October through the turn of the year, Spwashi keeps the studio on lore.land as its resident artist. The season has three terms and a round-out. Each term keeps the same three closes, so a reader always knows when to look again.

*article_i

Article I. The terms

  1. First term · October · Ink

    A mark a day. Pieces are drawn, inked, and scanned in batches; thirty-one by the month's last day.

    Leaves behind: a scanned set, and the first sheets of a new folio.

  2. Second term · November · Pantry

    Characters, places, and motifs from the ink term are named and shelved in the lore.land pantry, where a homebrew table can reach for them.

    Leaves behind: reference entries, each with its picture and its source.

  3. Third term · December · Lantern

    The strongest pieces are finished, laminated, and hung for the season's end. Guests' pieces hang beside them, credited.

    Leaves behind: a folio edition for the year.

  4. Round-out · the turn of the year · Threshold

    One card crosses from the season into the next: what the residency made, what carries forward, and what it will ask of next year. It is written on the new-year seed card.

    Leaves behind: the charter for next season, amended.

.article_ii

Article II. The closes

Every term closes three times: on the 13th, on the 26th, and on its last day. At each close the resident posts a receipt on Now: what was made, what was scanned, what moved into the pantry. A close is kept even when the receipt is short.

&article_iii

Article III. Guests

Anyone may bring a piece, a scene, or a world to the residency with the collaboration card. A guest's work is credited to the guest. It enters the pantry as a reference entry only when it fits the world and the guest wants it there.

~article_iv

Article IV. What is kept every year

A tradition is a structure agreed to before anyone knows what it will hold. This charter keeps four:

  1. The season opens with a mark on October 1.
  2. Every term keeps its three closes.
  3. Guests are credited by name, every time.
  4. The round-out card is written, and the charter is amended from it.

Everything else may change from season to season. These four are how a returning reader recognizes the place.

What this demo decides, and what it does not

It tries a residency as a charter: numbered articles, named terms, and closes that repeat, played straight so the structure itself carries the delight. It does not decide the term names, whether the residency happens, what a guest receives beyond credit, or where the charter would live if it did.
