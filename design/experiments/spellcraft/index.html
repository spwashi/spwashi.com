4. Decompose walkthrough (the gated demo)

  1. Hold-prime any living term or brace on a content route to gather ingredients.
  2. Save a working set from the spell dock (checkpoint).
  3. Press $ decompose on the saved bundle card: its registry re-enters the cauldron through spell:capture.
  4. Confirm the vessel phase bundle advances and the ledger records spell-decomposed.

This walkthrough is the promotion gate for the decompose verb: it must feel like reopening a draft, not replaying a log.

5. Wander spells (QA as a walk)

The team checks the site by walking it. Each spell below is one walk: a place to go, one thing to carry, one verb to cast on arrival, and the crack this path tends to find. The seed is the spell's name, so two people who cast the same spell walk the same ground and can compare what they saw.

  1. lamina — carry a scanned page from the stack to the front door.
    walk
    /design/folios/#release-set, then home, where the No. 20 folio waits beside the name
    hold
    an image: one folio scan, paper sealed under its laminate
    cast
    Find a prompt
    notice
    a scan cropped to fill a frame, a laminate with no rim, or a prompt that describes a render when what you held was paper
    sense
    npm run sense -- wander lamina · no still yet
    attention kept
    walked again whenever the weekly five turn over, so the page it checks is always this week's paper
  2. wash — read the recipe hero after dark.
    walk
    /recipes/#recipe-field with the dark palette on
    hold
    an image: the cream-and-ochre wash behind the kitchen's opening
    cast
    Nourish
    notice
    ink that collapses into the darkened wash, so a heading reads as texture instead of words
    sense
    npm run sense -- wander wash · still recipes-hero-dark
    attention kept
    the climate chapter of the review; walked after any change to a theme pack or the dark palette
  3. seats — sit in each lens seat and carry two views of one component.
    walk
    /design/components/#lens-seats
    hold
    a component variant: turn a seat's lens, hold the panel it reveals, turn it again, hold the second
    cast
    Mix, with both variants of the same component in the vessel
    notice
    a variant that only exists once scripts run, a seat whose pressed state disappears in high contrast, or a mix that cannot tell the two views apart
    sense
    npm run sense -- wander seats · no still yet
    attention kept
    whoever changes the lens owner or the variant marks walks this before the change lands
  4. siblings — read the kitchen's notes at genre depth, one paragraph at a time.
    walk
    /recipes/#recipe-field-note down through the sensory and technique notes
    hold
    a term: culture day, then one genre-depth note beside it
    cast
    Mix
    notice
    a genre sibling duplicated: two notes telling the same kitchen twice, or a genre note standing beside its plainer twin when only one should show
    sense
    npm run sense -- wander siblings · no still yet
    attention kept
    the copy pass that edits one note in a cluster walks the rest of its siblings in the same sitting
  5. pocket — open the site from a phone's home screen and keep a trail.
    walk
    home, installed and opened standalone, at pocket width
    hold
    an image and a component: the No. 20 folio preview, then the ingredient chip in its corner
    cast
    Save trail
    notice
    a standalone crop that shrinks the folio to a stamp, chips crowding one corner, or a saved trail coming back into the pot as a new ingredient
    sense
    npm run sense -- wander pocket · one still: home, with the ingredient chip opened
    attention kept
    walked on a real phone at each release threshold, by whoever cut the release
  6. anchor — follow a highlighted spell word with scripts off, then on.
    walk
    /topics/search/#spell-ingredients-title
    hold
    a term: anchor spell
    cast
    Nourish
    notice
    a highlighted word that promises meaning and, with scripts off, gives neither a definition nor a way home
    sense
    npm run sense -- wander anchor and npm run audit:living-terms · no still yet
    attention kept
    stays on this list until its word carries the creator's own definition; then it is walked with the living-terms census

Every wander leaves a receipt in the wander batch: seed, route, what was held, what was cast, what was noticed. A spell nobody has walked in a season is due; walking it again is the upkeep that keeps it on this list.
