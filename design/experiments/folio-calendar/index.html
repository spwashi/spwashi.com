October, one folio a day

Thirty-one scanned folios on October 2026's thirty-one days, in scan order. The grid comes from the date: the month starts on a Thursday, so three cells stay empty. Each day opens its folio on the shelf. Print it for a month on the wall.

What this demo decides, and what it does not

It decides that a month can be drawn from the date and the release set with no script. It does not decide the order: in scan order, Octvember (No. 09) lands on the 10th and the blue wash (No. 30) on Halloween. Chosen days would be a second demo. Approve one in a browser before this becomes a route.
