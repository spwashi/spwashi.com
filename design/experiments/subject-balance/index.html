The physics being demonstrated

  • & alone is a radical: one unpaired valence, so the expression carries charge and tilts toward its missing operand.
  • Hover is photon passage: a quantum of meaning previews the binding without consuming anything — leave, and nothing changed.
  • Click is a bond: the right operand commits, and the pair table proposes the left partner that balances it.
  • < & > is closure: both operands present, enclosure complete, charge grounded — the settle animation is the physics resolving.
