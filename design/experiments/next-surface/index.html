The next surface, beside this one

One piece of Home is set twice: first as the site draws it now, then as a proposed surface drawn from the premium and common concept boards. Nothing on any other page changes. The proposal has its own stylesheet, next-surface.css, written to stand alone, and every colour pair it uses for text meets AA contrast. The reasoning is in design-direction-2026-11.spw.

  • One focal point. A deep field holds the mark at scale and the title; reading sits on paper below it.
  • Three voices, one job each. Serif reads and names, sans carries controls, mono is kept for Spw lines.
  • Actions look like what they do. The look follows what the element does in HTML: the button that sends your line to be read is filled, a page here is outlined, another site is a quiet link that names the host. Each can say what travels with you.
  • Doors say the room in words. The operator rides along as a small mark that a screen reader skips.
