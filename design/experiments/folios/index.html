Folio experiments

Each of these draws on the scanned folios and asks one question of them. None is a route yet: approve or redirect it in a browser first.

  • Folio primes, frame by frame: one stock of hand-lettered cutouts rearranged twenty-five times on a clear sheet, stepped in scan order. Follow a cutout, choose a ground.
  • October, one folio a day: the September 26 set on October's thirty-one days, the grid drawn from the date.
  • A folio a day: one scanned piece and the question it asks, chosen from the date, the same for every reader.
