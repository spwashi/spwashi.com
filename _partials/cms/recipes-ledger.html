You salt a 2-quart stock until it tastes balanced, then simmer it down to 1 cup of demi-glace. How salty is that final cup?

*precipitate · Reveal the invariant

*precipitate — Concentration scales 1:1 with volume loss (8x)

Two quarts is 8 cups. Water boils at 100°C; sodium chloride boils at 1,465°C. Every milligram of salt you put into the 8-cup pot is still sitting inside that 1-cup reduction — only now gelatin coats the tongue so the spike hits half a second late.

CARD 01 · EMULSION

Starch water + cold fat = gloss without cream

3 tbsp pasta water : 2 tbsp cold butter : off-heat toss

Pull the skillet off the burner before the butter goes in, splash starchy water first, and shake hard enough to hear a wet slap instead of a sizzle. Sizzle means the fat is splitting; slap means droplets are suspending.

Amylose leached into boiling water coats sheared fat globules as a steric stabilizer; above ~78°C milk solids flocculate and coalesce back into slick grease.

Save winter squash peels and leek tops in the freezer bag beside the stockpot — mineral-rich broth emulsifies twice as cleanly as tap water.

Hold pasta ninety seconds shy of al dente before guests sit; the ninety-second finish in the pan is the entire show and plates hot.

CARD 02 · FERMENT

Two percent salt by total weight — water plus vegetable

1000 g chopped cabbage + water : 20 g fine sea salt : 18–21°C

Weigh the empty jar first, tare it, pack the vegetables and cover with water, then multiply that combined gram weight by 0.02. Brining only the water under-salts dense roots and stalls the souring.

2.0% w/w NaCl selectively suppresses aerobic spoilage flora while Leuconostoc mesenteroides drops pH below 4.6, handing the baton to acid-tolerant Lactobacillus plantarum.

Outer cabbage leaves carry the wild lactic bloom straight from the bed; rinse mud gently, never scrub with chlorinated hot water, and use one whole outer leaf as the submerged follower.

Label the lid with start date and gram tare on masking tape — a jar opened on Wednesday night turns plain rice and roast pork into a feast in thirty seconds.
