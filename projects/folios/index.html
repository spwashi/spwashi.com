Projects / Laminated folios

Laminated folios

I make laminated folios as a meditative practice: collage and laminate what is current, paint a season across the stack, then paint the same surfaces again the next year. The folio shelf holds them. This page tells how they came onto the site.

How it works

Each piece is scanned at 300 dpi. The master stays off the site; the site keeps up to three sizes in two formats and a small .spw record that names the piece, its scan, and how it may be used. Five scans open at full size each week.

Touch it live

Walk the shelf, read where the scans live, or ask for a piece.

What scanning taught

Putting a held thing online meant deciding what one scan is.

  • A face is not a piece. The record says it plainly: "A panel id names one scanned face, not a panel." Which front belongs to which back is not yet written down.
  • Every picture stands on something you could hold. The piece, its master scan, and the public sizes are three resting places, and the shelf marks its scans "scanned, not generated."
  • A path can be open before anything walks it. The shelf describes how a picture grown from a scan would link back to it. None has been made yet.
