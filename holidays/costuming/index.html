The costume sheet

A character sheet for a costume, kept the way a character sheet is: one line each, filled in as far as you mean it.

@ who you appeared as: the figure, character, or name. A costume, a character, a voice.
~ where you met it, who taught it: where the costume or the practice came from, and what is not yours to claim.
^ what it let you say: what you said or made that you would not have otherwise.
. what stayed: what remained after the night. It may be more than you expected.
